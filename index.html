— Rules & Check-in —

Before kickoff

The essentials from the 2026 tournament rules. Tap a topic to open it.

01Registration & eligibility
  • Open to properly registered youth teams from all U.S. Soccer–affiliated organizations (USYS, US Club, FYSA, etc.), in good standing with their association.
  • Each team presents an official stamped roster, permission to travel (if required) and proof of medical insurance.
  • Coaches and players need current laminated passes with photo, team code and date of birth. A player may play for one team only.
02Guest players & check-in
  • Up to 5 guest/loan players per team (club-pass players don't count), all from the same membership platform as the team (USYS or US Club).
  • Check-in documents are uploaded to your GotSport team account. por confirmar: check-in online obligatorio en 2026
03Format by age
  • U9–U10: 7v7 (min. 5), 2 × 25 min, roster max. 12, ball size 4.
  • U11–U12: 9v9 (min. 6), 2 × 30 min, roster max. 16, ball size 4.
  • U13–U19: 11v11 (min. 7), 2 × 35 min, roster max. 18, ball size 5.
  • No deliberate heading for U12 and younger.
04Game day
  • Report to the field 30 minutes before kickoff for player-pass verification. A registered coach with a valid pass must be in the technical area.
  • Teams without the minimum number of eligible players at kickoff forfeit 4-0.
05Format & points
  • World Cup–style pool play with a minimum of three games per team.
  • Win 3 · Tie 1 · Loss 0. No overtime: ties in elimination rounds go to penalty kicks.
06Awards & refunds
  • Champions and finalists receive team trophies and individual medals right after the final.
  • No refunds after acceptance, except tournament cancellation or failure to bracket a team.
