— Check-in & Rules —

Before kickoff

Online check-in is mandatory. Tap a topic to open it.

01Online check-in
  • Check-in is online and mandatory: upload every form to your GotSport team account. You can upload documents as soon as you register.
  • No onsite check-in. To add a player after check-in, upload a new roster and email the tournament.
02Rosters & guest players
  • Accepted rosters: US Club, FYSA/USYS state-generated rosters and AYSO rosters. League rosters (SFU, FLUGSA, CDL, GCF, etc.) are not accepted.
  • Guest players: up to 4 for U9–U12 and up to 6 for U13 and up, with the guest player form attached.
03Waivers & passes
  • Every rostered player needs a completed and signed waiver.
  • All coaches and players need valid, verified passes with signatures and picture, in roster order.
04Game day
  • Teams go straight to their field; coaches hand the referee the passes and the approved roster.
  • Be at the field 30 minutes before kickoff. Teams arriving 15 minutes after kickoff may forfeit.
05Format & points
  • World Cup–style pool play with a minimum of three games per team.
  • Win 3 · Tie 1 · Loss 0. No overtime: ties in elimination rounds go to penalty kicks.
06Awards & refunds
  • Champions and finalists receive team trophies and individual medals right after the final.
  • No refunds after acceptance, except tournament cancellation or failure to bracket a team.
